# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

SyncFlo is for iPhone and iPad users who pay with Apple Pay and want a fast, private expense log without connecting a bank, reading notifications, or handing a third party their transaction history. They are comfortable setting up one Shortcuts Wallet transaction automation in exchange for low-friction capture later.

## Product Purpose

SyncFlo turns a user-created Wallet transaction automation in Shortcuts into a clean, local-first expense ledger. Success means a purchase can become a useful expense row quickly, duplicates are avoided, uncertain imports are easy to review, and privacy boundaries stay obvious.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, trustworthy, lucid. The brand should feel like a pocket ledger made for daylight use: calm, immediate, native-feeling, and careful with claims.

## Anti-references

Avoid crypto or neon finance styling, over-gamified budgeting, corporate banking dashboards, generic purple AI SaaS visuals, decorative frosted-glass effects, hype-heavy automation language, and claims that imply bank aggregation, notification reading, Wallet scraping, or hosted AI.

## Design Principles

- Show the privacy boundary before asking for trust.
- Make the app feel close to the phone, not like a generic web finance dashboard.
- Let the real product screens carry the proof.
- Keep copy specific: Wallet automation, local ledger, review states, optional BYO-key AI.
- Position Pro as planning and insight, while making it clear that capture stays free.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, daylight-readable text, responsive layouts from mobile to desktop, keyboard-accessible navigation, descriptive alt text for product imagery, and reduced-motion alternatives for animated elements.
